# Swedish traffic sign illustrations

These six unmodified PNG files are the official web illustrations published by
Transportstyrelsen. The agency states that its road signs may be used freely
without approval and asks that they be used in their proper context. Source
image URLs, semantic meanings, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`manifest.json`. Filenames include a content hash so browser and hosting caches
cannot silently keep an older sign drawing.

Do not substitute international sign variants: Swedish B2 is a red octagon
with `STOP`, B3 is a blue square pedestrian-crossing sign, A40 is a yellow
triangle with a red border and exclamation mark, D4 is a blue circle with a
white bicycle, A15 is a yellow triangle with two children, and C31-3 is a yellow
circle with a red border and black `30`.

The signs are used here as educational examples: B2 stop, B3 pedestrian
crossing, A40 general warning, D4 cycle path, A15 children and C31 30 km/h.

Source and use terms: <https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/om-oss/pressrum/pressbilder/>
